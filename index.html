Ambient Room

The screen glows green, then amber, then red as the meeting uses energy. After the planned time, the glow pulses and dims.

Researcher keys (Wizard of Oz):
Space = pause / resume · T = show/hide energy text
M = mark an event in the log · S = sound on/off
+ / − = add / remove 1 minute of planned time
[ / ] = clap detection less / more sensitive
E = end the meeting and download the log (CSV)
